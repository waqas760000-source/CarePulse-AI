End-to-End Orchestration

The Four Steps to Zero Emergency Delay

How CarePulse AI transforms the emergency continuum from first contact to discharge.

01

Intake & Sensor Feed

Patient arrives via ambulance or walk-in. Vitals, past medical history, and chief complaint are ingested through bedside monitors or triage tablets.

02

AI Acuity Stratification

The neural engine evaluates life threats, computes Shock Index, determines ESI score 1-5, and suggests targeted diagnostic lab orders.

03

Optimal Bed Assignment

CarePulse AI dynamically pairs the patient with the nearest appropriate bed bay (Resus, Trauma, Acute, FastTrack) balancing clinician load.

04

Ambient SBAR Charting

Physician bedside discussion is parsed into structured clinical documentation, synchronized instantly to the hospital's EHR via FHIR APIs.
