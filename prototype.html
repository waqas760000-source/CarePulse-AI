Technical Specifications & Live System Demo

Interactive Application Prototype: Clinical Flow & Triage Simulation

CarePulse AI is a multi-modular clinical intelligence platform. Below you will find both the comprehensive technical design documentation and an interactive, client-side simulation engine demonstrating the four core algorithmic pipelines.

1. Clinical Acuity Engine

Implements the standardized 5-tier Emergency Severity Index (ESI) algorithm combined with automated Shock Index calculations (HR / Systolic BP) and SIRS criteria to instantly detect occult instability.

2. Dynamic Ward Orchestration

Continuously monitors bed status across Resuscitation bays, Trauma suites, Acute treatment zones, and ambulatory Fast-Track clinics, dynamically dispatching patients to minimize boarding.

3. Ambient NLP & FHIR EMR

Transforms spoken bedside conversations into standardized clinical SBAR notes, eliminating hours of manual typing and generating FHIR Observation & Encounter JSON bundles.
