Clinical Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

CarePulse AI™ Comprehensive User Guide & Manual

A complete operational guide for emergency medicine personnel, hospital bed managers, clinical informatics directors, and IT integration engineers.

ROLE 01 • TRIAGE NURSE PROTOCOL

Rapid Intake, Acuity Verification & Primary Allocation

The primary goal of the Triage Nurse is to capture vital parameters within 60 seconds of arrival, verify the AI-computed ESI score, and prevent waiting-room deterioration.

Step 1: Ingest & Scan

Tap bedside monitor barcode or swipe patient health card. Vitals auto-populate into CarePulse AI tablet.

Step 2: Acuity Review

CarePulse AI outputs ESI Level 1-5, Shock Index, and high-risk flags (Sepsis, STEMI, Occult Stroke).

Step 3: Confirm & Dispatch

Accept suggested bay or override with one touch. Notification sounds immediately at assigned bay.

💡 Clinical Pro-Tip for Triage Nurses:

If a patient's Heart Rate divided by Systolic BP exceeds 0.9 (Shock Index Alert), the system will highlight the patient card in flashing amber even if their mental status appears normal. Never demote a high Shock Index without attending physician concurrence.

ROLE 02 • EMERGENCY ATTENDING PHYSICIAN

Bedside Evaluation, Ambient SBAR & Order Execution

CarePulse AI functions as your ambient clinical scribe and differential diagnostic co-pilot, letting you focus on the patient instead of keyboard typing.

1. Differential Audit

Review the top 3 AI differential probabilities and recommended diagnostic bundle (e.g. Troponin, CT, Ultrasound).

2. Ambient Dictation

Speak naturally with the patient. Ambient AI acoustic filters extract chief complaint, history, and physical findings.

3. Sign SBAR Note

Review auto-generated SBAR note on tablet, tap "Authorize", and commit note directly to Epic/Cerner chart.

ROLE 03 • ED CHARGE NURSE & BED COORDINATOR

Capacity Balancing, Turnaround & Surge Management

The central dashboard provides total operational visibility across all ED beds, waiting room queues, and inpatient hospital handoffs.

  • Discharge Lounge Routing: Flag stable patients who have orders completed to the discharge lounge, freeing acute bays 45 minutes faster.
  • Fast Track Flexibility: When ESI-4/5 volume surges past 20 patients, toggle "FastTrack Surge Expansion" to repurpose consultation rooms.
  • Ambulance Diversion Threshold: Automated alerts notify regional dispatchers before hospital reaches 95% capacity, preventing ambulance pile-ups.

HL7 FHIR v4 Interoperability & Security Enclave

CarePulse AI connects via standard SMART on FHIR OAuth 2.0 gateways with zero disruption to legacy EHR databases.

Resolution: Nurse Clinical Override Protocol

Governing Principle: Clinician judgment always supercedes AI suggestion. Tap the "Acuity Override" pencil icon next to the ESI score, select the revised score (e.g. adjust from ESI-3 to ESI-2), and choose the clinical justification from the dropdown (e.g., "Severe diaphoresis / clinical intuition").

✔ The system will immediately re-queue the patient and log the override for continuous machine learning reinforcement.
